##1. The Idea

MatchNstudy is a website I created to help university students find people to study with who are taking the same courses and have similar schedules. As a university student, I know it can be difficult to find someone to study with, especially when you don't know many people in your classes. A lot of students end up studying alone or relying on friends and group chats, which doesn't always work when everyone has different courses and schedules.

My idea is to make finding study partners easier by having everything in one place. Students can search for others based on their courses, availability, and study preferences. Instead of having to ask around, they can find people who are working on the same material and want to study together.

I would start MatchNstudy at St. Francis Xavier University and eventually expand to other universities if it becomes successful. The website would be free to use for basic matching, with optional paid features such as priority matching and creating more study groups. This would allow students to use the main features without paying while giving the business a way to make money.

#2 IT business Value

MatchNstudy uses information technology to make finding study partners easier and more convenient for university students. Normally, students have to ask people in their classes, message group chats, or rely on friends to find someone to study with. This can take time, especially when everyone has different schedules.

By using a website, MatchNstudy brings everything together in one place. Students can search for people taking the same courses and filter potential study partners based on their availability and preferences. This reduces the time spent looking for someone and makes it easier to find a suitable match.

Technology also allows MatchNstudy to reach more students without needing a physical location. As the website grows, more students could join and find study partners from different courses and universities. The website could also be improved over time by adding features such as group scheduling, messaging, and personalized recommendations.

Overall, the main business value of MatchNstudy is that it uses technology to solve a common student problem in a faster and more organized way.

#3 Network Effects

Network effects are important to MatchNstudy because the website becomes more useful as more students join. For example, if only five students are using the website, there might not be anyone taking the same course or available at the same time. However, if hundreds of students join, there would be a much better chance of finding a compatible study partner.

MatchNstudy would benefit from direct network effects because students gain more value when other students join the platform. As the number of users increases, there would be more potential matches, study groups, and opportunities to connect with classmates.

Another challenge with network effects is getting the first group of students to sign up. If students visit MatchNstudy and cannot find anyone in their classes, they might not return. To prevent this, I would focus on promoting the website to students taking popular first-year courses because these classes usually have larger numbers of students. This could help create more matches early on. I would also encourage students who successfully find study partners to recommend the website to their friends. Over time, this could help MatchNstudy grow through word of mouth instead of relying entirely on paid advertising.

One challenge would be attracting enough students when the website first launches. Students might not want to sign up if there aren't many people to match with. To help solve this problem, I would initially promote MatchNstudy at StFX through student social media pages, class group chats, and word of mouth. Starting at one university would help build a larger group of users in the same location before expanding. I would also create scanning codes throughout campus as I have seen before for surveys.

Overall, network effects could help MatchNstudy grow because more students joining would make the website more valuable and encourage others to use it.

#4 VRIN Analysis

VRIN is used to evaluate whether a business has resources or capabilities that could give it a competitive advantage.

Valuable: I believe MatchNstudy is valuable because it helps students solve a common problem. Instead of spending time asking classmates or searching through group chats, students can use the website to find people taking similar courses who are available to study.

Rare: MatchNstudy could be somewhat rare because it focuses specifically on connecting students based on their university courses and schedules. Although there are other ways to find study partners, having these features together in one place could make the website stand out.

Inimitable: One weakness of MatchNstudy is that the website itself would not be very difficult for another company to copy. However, if the platform became popular at StFX, its existing community of students and study groups could become harder for competitors to recreate.

Non-substitutable: MatchNstudy is not completely non-substitutable because students can still find study partners through friends, class group chats, or social media. However, the website could offer a more organized and convenient alternative.

Overall, I think MatchNstudy has the potential to develop a competitive advantage, but it would need to build a strong student community and continue improving its features to stay competitive.

#5 Cloud Technology and Operations 

Cloud technology is important to MatchNstudy because it allows me to create and operate a website without having to purchase expensive equipment or maintain my own servers.

For this project, I used GitHub to store my website files and GitHub Codespaces to work on the website online. I also used GitHub Pages to publish my website so that other people can access it through a link. This made it possible to create a working prototype without spending money on web hosting.

One advantage of cloud technology is that it allows the business to start small and potentially grow over time. If MatchNstudy became popular at other universities, I would eventually need more advanced hosting and a database to manage student accounts, schedules, and matches.

Another benefit is that I can make changes to the website without having to manage physical servers. However, relying on online services also creates risks, such as website outages and security concerns.

Overall, cloud technology makes MatchNstudy more affordable and easier to manage as a student starting a business. It also gives the business the opportunity to expand if more students begin using the platform.

#6 Risks and Ethics

One of the biggest risks for MatchNstudy would be protecting students' personal information. Since students would potentially share their names, courses, schedules, and study preferences, it would be important to keep this information secure. If I developed the website into a real business, I would need to introduce secure student accounts, privacy settings, and proper data protection.

Another risk would be students creating fake profiles or using the platform inappropriately. To help prevent this, I would consider university email verification and a reporting system so students could report suspicious accounts or inappropriate behaviour.

Another issue I would need to consider is how students interact with each other in person. Since MatchNstudy is designed to help people find study partners, some students might arrange to meet someone they have never met before. I would encourage students to meet in public locations such as the university library or other campus study spaces. I would also want the website to provide clear safety guidelines so students understand how to protect themselves.

There is also a financial risk because MatchNstudy might not attract enough students to become successful. If there are not enough users, students might struggle to find matches and stop using the website. I would try to reduce this risk by launching at StFX first and keeping the basic features free.

I would also need to consider fairness and accessibility. The website should be easy to use and available to students regardless of whether they pay for premium features.

Overall, I believe these risks could be managed through careful planning, protecting student information, and listening to feedback from users.
