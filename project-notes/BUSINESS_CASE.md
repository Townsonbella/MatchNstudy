## 1. The Website

MatchNstudy is a website I created to help university students find people to study with who are taking the same courses and have similar schedules. As a university student, I know it can be difficult to find someone to study with, especially when you don't know many individuals in your classes. A lot of people end up studying alone, which isn't a bad thing or relying on friends and group chats, which doesn't always work when everyone has different courses and schedules, as I have learned.

My idea is to make finding study partners easier by having everything in one place, connecting people and studies. Students can search for others based on their courses, availability, and study preferences. Instead of having to ask around, they can find people who are working on the same material and want to study together. I found that working in groups and with people who can all place ideas and understandings together so we can all succeed to the best of our ability while helping each other.

I would start MatchNstudy at St. Francis Xavier University and eventually expand to other universities with higher student counts, if it becomes successful. The website would be free to use for basic matching, with optional paid features such as priority matching and creating more study groups. This would allow students to use the main features without paying while giving the business a way to make more money.

## 2 IT business Value

MatchNstudy uses information technology to make finding study partners easier and more convenient for university students. Normally, students have to ask people in their classes, message group chats, or rely on friends to find someone to study with. This can take time, especially when everyone has different schedules and helps manage planning. I find this especially useful since I have a hard time managing my time and priorities. 

By using a website such as this one, MatchNstudy brings everything together by using databases. Students can search for people taking the same courses and filter potential study partners based on their availability and preferences. This reduces the time spent looking for someone and makes it easier to find a suitable match.

Technology also allows MatchNstudy to reach more students without needing a physical location. As the website grows, more students could join and find study partners from different courses and universities. The website could also be improved over time by adding features such as group scheduling, messaging, and personalized recommendations in order to consolidate information and people all together. even adding on features of group calls where people can compare study notes and test reviews. Bringing up the collective whole with the use of technology.

The main business value of MatchNstudy is that it uses technology to solve a common student problem in a faster and more organized way.

## 3 Network Effects

Network effects are important to MatchNstudy because the website becomes more useful as more students join. For example, if only five students are using the website, there might not be anyone taking the same course or available at the same time. However, if hundreds of students join, there would be a much better chance of finding a compatible study partner, increasing the value and time for all students within the website.

MatchNstudy would benefit from direct network effects because students gain more value when other students join the platform. As the number of users increases, there would be more potential matches, study groups, and opportunities to connect with classmates. Additionally, the more people can be helped, and in general do better within their classes.

Another challenge with network effects is getting the first group of students to sign up. If students visit MatchNstudy and cannot find anyone in their classes, they might not return. To prevent this, I would focus on promoting the website to students taking popular first-year courses because these classes usually have larger numbers of students such as the mandatory prerequisites. This could help create more matches early on . Over time, this could help MatchNstudy grow through word of mouth instead of relying entirely on paid advertising that would be evenly funded by paid features on the app. As well as asking teachers to share the word and go to organizations like Trimble who already host group study sessions.
Additionally, I would initially promote MatchNstudy at StFX through student social media pages, class group chats, and word of mouth. Starting at one university would help build a larger group of users in the same location before expanding. I would also create scanning codes throughout campus, as I have seen before for surveys. 

Therefore, network effects could help MatchNstudy grow because there would be a domino effect as more students joining would make the website more valuable and encourage others to use it.

## 4 VRIN Analysis

VRIN is used to evaluate whether a business has resources or capabilities that could give it a competitive advantage.

**Valuable**: I believe MatchNstudy is valuable because it helps students solve a common problem. Instead of spending time asking classmates or searching through group chats, students can use the website to find people taking similar courses who are available to study. It would be very valuable, especially to me as a first mover placing an attempt to bring everyone together.

**Rare**: Match study could be somewhat rare because it focuses specifically on connecting students based on their university courses and schedules. Although there are other ways to find study partners, having these features together in one place could make the website stand out.

**Inimitable**: One weakness of MatchNstudy is that the website itself would not be very difficult for another company to copy. However, if the platform became popular at StFX, its existing community of students and study groups could become harder for competitors to recreate. If I focused on staying power and consumer loyalty, it could help with the inimitability of the website.

**Non-substitutable**: MatchNstudy is not completely non-substitutable because students can still find study partners through friends, class group chats, or social media. However, the website could offer a more organized and convenient alternative to finding someone to study with instantly and be available to anyone, even if they struggle to find friends and peers within the same course.

In general, I think MatchNstudy has the potential to develop a competitive advantage, but it would need to build a strong student community and continue improving its features to stay competitive. The main hope would be to bring everyone together and they like the website so they won't feel the need to use any alternative website.

## 6 Risks and Ethics

One of the biggest risks for MatchNstudy would be protecting students' personal information. Since students would potentially share their names, courses, schedules, and study preferences, it would be important to keep this information secure. If I developed the website into a real business, I would need to introduce secure student accounts, privacy settings, and proper data protection. Overall, proper IT security like firewalls.

Another risk would be students creating fake profiles or using the platform inappropriately. To help prevent this, I would consider university email verification like two-factor authentication and a reporting system so students could report suspicious accounts or inappropriate behaviour in order to get banned and taken off the platform.I would implement a zero tolerance program. 

Another issue I would need to consider is how students interact with each other in person. Since MatchNstudy is designed to help people find study partners, some students might arrange to meet someone they have never met before. I would encourage students to meet in public locations such as the university library or other campus study spaces. I would also want the website to provide clear safety guidelines so students understand how to protect themselves. I would alsotry to reduce risk by launching at StFX first and keeping the basic features free or even offering premium trials like on Duolingo.

I would also need to consider fairness and accessibility. The website should be easy to use and available to students regardless of whether they pay for premium features.

 I believe these risks could be managed through careful planning, protecting student information, and listening to feedback from users.
